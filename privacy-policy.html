Art History Note Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled in Art History Note (the "App").

Summary
  • We do not ask you to create an account and do not collect your name, email, or phone number.
  • Your visit notebook (saved artworks, visit dates and check marks) and your settings are stored only on your device and are not sent to us.
  • The App shows ads through Google AdMob, which may collect device identifiers and usage data as described below.

1. Information stored on your device

The App's art content (movements, artists, artworks, museums and artwork images) is built into the App. Browsing and searching it does not send anything to us.

The following information is created by you and stored only on your device. It is not stored on our servers, and we do not have access to it.

FeatureData storedPurpose
Visit notebookMuseums you added, artworks you want to see, visit dates, artworks checked during a visitPlan museum visits and keep visit records
SettingsDisplay mode (light / dark), app languageRemember your preferences
SearchSearch words you typeShow results on screen. Search words are not saved or sent.

The App does not request access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos or files. Deleting the App deletes all of the information above from your device.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App displays banner ads and a native ad on the exit screen, provided by Google AdMob. Google and its partners may collect and use the following information to serve and measure ads:

  • Advertising ID (Android Advertising ID)
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device information (model, OS version, language)
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks)

For more information, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy.

You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device's Settings > Google > Ads (or Settings > Privacy > Ads).

3. Data retention and sharing

We do not store personal data on our own servers. Data collected by Google AdMob is retained according to Google's policies. We do not sell or share personal data with any third party other than Google as described above.

4. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

6. Contact
